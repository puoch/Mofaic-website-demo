# Brand files

`coat-of-arms.svg` is the coat of arms of the Republic of South Sudan, used as
the site emblem at the sponsor's request (29 October 2026, with reference to
https://op.gov.ss/). This copy is the vector drawing from the `coat-of-arms`
npm package 6.4.0 (MIT; drawing originally from Wikimedia Commons), minified
with svgo. Replace it with the file the Ministry supplies before launch.

Set `Config\Branding::$emblem` to change or hide the emblem.

`flag-south-sudan.svg` is the national flag of South Sudan, supplied by the
sponsor on 10 November 2026 for the "About South Sudan" tile on the home page,
where it sits under a 60% blue overlay.

`eservices-emblem.png` is the eServices emblem, supplied by the sponsor on
10 November 2026 for the "eServices" tile on the home page, which opens
https://accounts.eservices.gov.ss/mda/service/3 in a new tab. It sits whole on
the right of the tile under the same 60% blue overlay; the title and text stay
at least 7:1 against it.
