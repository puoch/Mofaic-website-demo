# Brand files

`coat-of-arms.svg` is the coat of arms of the Republic of South Sudan, used as
the site emblem at the sponsor's request (29 October 2026, with reference to
https://op.gov.ss/). Since 10 November 2026 this copy is the SVG the sponsor
supplied for the staff sign-in page (the same Wikimedia Commons drawing as the
earlier `coat-of-arms` npm 6.4.0 copy). It was checked for scripts, event
handlers and external links (none), cleaned of editor metadata and three
broken `<use>` references, and minified with svgo 3.3.2. It is shown in the
header, the footer and centred above the staff sign-in form. Replace it with
the file the Ministry supplies before launch.

Set `Config\Branding::$emblem` to change or hide the emblem.

`flag-south-sudan.svg` is the national flag of South Sudan, supplied by the
sponsor on 10 November 2026 for the "About South Sudan" tile on the home page,
where it sits under a 60% blue overlay.

`eservices-emblem.png` is the eServices emblem, supplied by the sponsor on
10 November 2026 for the "eServices" tile on the home page, which opens
https://accounts.eservices.gov.ss/mda/service/3 in a new tab. It sits whole on
the right of the tile under the same 60% blue overlay; the title and text stay
at least 7:1 against it.

`diplomatic-protocol-flags.jpg` is a photo of national flags, supplied by the
sponsor on 10 November 2026 for the "Diplomatic Protocol" tile on the home page,
which links to the MFA & IC Diplomatic Protocol page. It fills the tile under
the same 60% blue overlay. It was re-saved for the web (570 x 360, 43 KB, no
metadata). The image looks like a stock-photo preview: confirm the Ministry
holds a licence for it, or replace it, before launch.
